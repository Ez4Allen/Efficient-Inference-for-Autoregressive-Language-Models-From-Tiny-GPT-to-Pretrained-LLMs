# Supporting and Legacy Experiments

The repository retains earlier course exercises:

- TinyGPT training and generation;
- GPT-2 prefill/decode benchmarking;
- request-scheduling simulation;
- plotting and general benchmark utilities.

These components demonstrate implementation history and provide sanity baselines, but they are not part of the final GameGuideLM research claim. The final project is defined by multi-game grounding, Qwen model adaptation, draft/target analysis, and speculative decoding.

# Terraria Guide Corpus Attribution

The guide corpus generated by this project is derived from textual content on
[The Official Terraria Wiki](https://terraria.wiki.gg/).

Wiki text that the site may lawfully license is available under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), unless a page
states otherwise.

Every imported document and retrieval chunk retains its source page URL,
revision identifier, retrieval timestamp, and license metadata. The importer
collects text only; images, audio, and other media are excluded because their
licenses may differ.

This corpus is intended for non-commercial research and educational use. Any
redistribution of derived text must preserve attribution and comply with the
same license terms.

# Stardew Valley guide corpus attribution

The guide pipeline imports text from the **Official Stardew Valley Wiki** through its MediaWiki API. Wiki text is licensed under **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0**. Page titles, URLs, revision IDs, retrieval timestamps, and license metadata are retained in every document and chunk.

The pipeline does not download or redistribute game images, sprites, audio, or other developer-owned assets. Generated raw pages, cleaned documents, chunks, reports, and SQLite databases are local build artifacts and are not tracked by Git.
